About Deliny Electronics Center

A Masaka electronics shop built on trust and good service.

Who we are

Deliny Electronics Center is an electronics shop in Masaka serving homes, schools, offices and small businesses across the region. We stock televisions, phones, computers, sound systems, solar equipment and the accessories that go with them.

We started with a simple idea: people should buy electronics from someone they can find again. Every item we sell comes with honest advice and a warranty.
